Beginner's Roadmap · 新手入门指南

How to Get Started in 3 Simple Steps 如何开始这项爱好：极简3步指引

You don't need extraordinary flexibility or expensive equipment to begin your practice today. 无需天生柔软的筋骨，也无需昂贵门槛，今天就能轻松迈出第一步。

01
🧘‍♂️

Step 1: Prepare a Cozy Space & Mat 第1步：准备舒适角落与瑜伽垫

Designate a quiet, well-ventilated 2x2 meter space at home. All you need is a non-slip yoga mat (around 5mm thickness) and comfortable, stretchy clothes that let you move freely. 在家中找一处通风安静、约两米见方的小空间。备好一张防滑瑜伽垫（建议厚度5毫米左右），换上宽松透气的弹性衣服，即可随时开始。

💡
Key takeaway: Keep the area clutter-free so your mind feels instantly relaxed. 核心要点： 保持空间整洁开阔，环境清爽心境自然沉静。
02
🌅

Step 2: Start with 10 Minutes of Daily Basics 第2步：从每日10分钟基础动作起步

Begin with gentle beginner poses such as Mountain Pose (Tadasana), Cat-Cow Stretch, and Child's Pose (Balasana). Never force yourself into uncomfortable twists or strain. 初学者切忌挑战高难度体式。从山式站立、猫牛式脊柱温和伸展以及婴儿式放松等温和体式练起，每天只要10分钟，循序渐进唤醒身体。

💡
Key takeaway: Yoga is about gentle self-care, not athletic competition. 核心要点： 瑜伽是自我关怀而非竞技，温柔倾听身体界限。
03
🌿

Step 3: Breathe Steadily & Build Consistency 第3步：伴随平稳呼吸，重在持之以恒

Harmonize each motion with slow nasal breathing. Practice 2–3 times a week; consistency will gradually build resilience, balance, and quiet confidence. 让每一次伸展顺应自然深缓的鼻吸鼻呼。每周坚持练习2至3次，放下对体式完美的执念，享受日复一日身心蜕变带来的踏实力量。

💡
Key takeaway: Consistency is the true magic—small daily habits lead to lifelong peace. 核心要点： 贵在坚持，微小而规律的练习终将汇聚成内心的充盈与安宁。
